# P26-005 — Verification Center workspace and live run experience

**Status:** `BLOCKED`  
**Depends on:** `P26-002`, `P26-004`  
**Authority:** `docs/roadmap/P26_VERIFICATION_CENTER.md`  
**Manifest:** `docs/roadmap/p26/manifest.v1.json`

## Objective

Build the end-user Verification Center workspace with live stages, criteria, commands,
logs, blockers, evidence, coverage and history.

## Scope

Implement only after P26-002 and P26-004. This is a general product workspace, not a
developer-only panel or a Kristin-specific screen.

## Deliverables

- Project/profile/criteria setup and run-launch experience.
- Live durable activity, criterion and evidence views.
- Exact report, coverage, history, cancellation and confirmation surfaces.

## Acceptance

- UI acknowledgement and first visible activity meet the governed budget.
- Cancel, retry and confirmation states are explicit and accessible.
- Developer internals are not the default UX and blockers are never visually presented as success.

## Evidence

Record golden/widget/integration evidence, accessibility checks, exact candidate identity,
performance measurements and truthful result states.

## Stop policy

Stop on dependency drift, hidden execution, inaccessible critical controls, stale report
binding, optimistic status rendering, source mismatch or performance-budget failure.
