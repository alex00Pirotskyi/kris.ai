# P26-007 — Bounded test-and-repair orchestration

**Status:** `BLOCKED`  
**Depends on:** `P26-002`  
**Authority:** `docs/roadmap/P26_VERIFICATION_CENTER.md`  
**Manifest:** `docs/roadmap/p26/manifest.v1.json`

## Objective

Implement authorized `TEST_AND_REPAIR` orchestration with an immutable attempt ledger and
a hard maximum of two repair attempts.

## Scope

Implement only after P26-002. Every change must stay inside governed scope, require any
applicable confirmation, and be reverified against exact source identity.

## Deliverables

- Durable attempt ledger with source-before/source-after identity.
- Diagnosis, authorization, change, affected recheck and stop-policy orchestration.
- Non-progress detection and hard attempt-budget enforcement.

## Acceptance

- Attempt three cannot be created through API, UI, retry or recovery paths.
- PASS, blocker, denied confirmation, scope violation or repeated non-progress stops early.
- Each change is source-bound, summarized and reverified with affected checks.

## Evidence

Record each attempt, authorization, exact source transition, change summary, selected
verification, timestamps, output/evidence references and result state.

## Stop policy

Stop on dependency drift, attempt-budget exhaustion, repeated non-progress, denied
authorization, permission/environment blocker, source mismatch or scope escape.
