# P26-006 — Managed execution, cancellation, logs and exact result states

**Status:** `BLOCKED`  
**Depends on:** `P26-004`  
**Authority:** `docs/roadmap/P26_VERIFICATION_CENTER.md`  
**Manifest:** `docs/roadmap/p26/manifest.v1.json`

## Objective

Bind verification plans to managed processes with ordered output, cancellation,
termination, evidence capture and exact six-state reports.

## Scope

Implement only after P26-004. Reuse the governed managed-process layer; do not introduce
shell-string execution or a second process lifecycle.

## Deliverables

- Managed argv execution with ordered activity and bounded logs.
- Cooperative cancel plus bounded termination fallback.
- Exact criterion, step and run aggregation with source/environment identity.

## Acceptance

- Governed profiles never execute shell strings.
- Cancellation and termination meet the governed budgets and leave a durable final state.
- Blocked, unknown and not-run results remain non-passing in every aggregation.

## Evidence

Record argv, process identity, output hashes/tails, cancellation timings, exact source and
environment identity, timestamps and final result state.

## Stop policy

Stop on dependency drift, unmanaged process creation, output reordering, orphan process,
source mismatch, state coercion or missing durable finalization.
