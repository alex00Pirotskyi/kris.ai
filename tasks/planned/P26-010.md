# P26-010 — Updater operation verification and rollback journey

**Status:** `BLOCKED`  
**Depends on:** `P26-003`  
**Authority:** `docs/roadmap/P26_VERIFICATION_CENTER.md`  
**Manifest:** `docs/roadmap/p26/manifest.v1.json`

## Objective

Verify P2 updater operations as distinct CHECK, DOWNLOAD, INSTALL, RESTART, VERIFY and
ROLLBACK stages with exact candidate identity and explicit authorization.

## Scope

Implement only after P26-003. Reuse P2 managed operations and updater contracts; do not
create a second updater or infer later-stage success from an earlier stage.

## Deliverables

- Stage-aware updater verification adapter and report model.
- Confirmation surfaces for install, restart and rollback boundaries.
- Exact post-operation verification and rollback evidence binding.

## Acceptance

- A PASS at any updater stage never implies PASS for a later unexecuted stage.
- Restart and rollback cannot execute without explicit confirmation.
- Rollback and post-restart evidence bind to the exact before/after candidate and environment.

## Evidence

Record updater operation and stage IDs, argv or governed action identity, exact candidate
and environment identity, confirmations, timestamps, bounded artifacts and result states.

## Stop policy

Stop on dependency drift, stage conflation, missing confirmation, candidate mismatch,
unsupported environment, stale evidence, unsafe rollback or evidence overclaim.
