# P26-004 — Structured, human and agent-prompt acceptance engine

**Status:** `BLOCKED`  
**Depends on:** `P26-002`  
**Authority:** `docs/roadmap/P26_VERIFICATION_CENTER.md`  
**Manifest:** `docs/roadmap/p26/manifest.v1.json`

## Objective

Implement first-class `STRUCTURED`, `HUMAN` and `AGENT_PROMPT` criterion records,
evaluation and fail-closed aggregation.

## Scope

Implement only after P26-002. Preserve authored text and provenance. Do not request,
store or expose hidden chain-of-thought; agent prompts ask for observable evidence.

## Deliverables

- Versioned criterion records and evaluators.
- Exact six-state criterion results with evidence links.
- Aggregation that treats only PASS as passing.

## Acceptance

- All criterion forms preserve provenance and authored content.
- BLOCKED_ENVIRONMENT, BLOCKED_PERMISSION, NOT_RUN and UNKNOWN never promote.
- Explanations expose evidence and limits without hidden reasoning.

## Evidence

Record criterion version, evaluator, exact source and environment identity, timestamps,
result state and evidence references.

## Stop policy

Stop on dependency drift, provenance loss, unsupported state creation, hidden-reasoning
capture, coercion of blockers or unknowns, or evidence mismatch.
