# P26-003 — Verification plan compiler and affected-test selection

**Status:** `BLOCKED`  
**Depends on:** `P26-001`  
**Authority:** `docs/roadmap/P26_VERIFICATION_CENTER.md`  
**Manifest:** `docs/roadmap/p26/manifest.v1.json`

## Objective

Compile acceptance criteria and project profiles into deterministic analyze, quick, deep
or repair plans with affected-test selection.

## Scope

Implement only after P26-001 is complete. Reuse canonical Test Center mapping and retain
explicit unknowns rather than inventing coverage.

## Deliverables

- Deterministic plan compiler and action preview.
- Affected-test selection with reasons and fallback policy.
- Confirmation and environment requirements attached to plan steps.

## Acceptance

- Identical inputs produce identical plans.
- Unknown mappings remain explicit and non-passing where evidence is required.
- Commands, actions, blockers and confirmations are previewable before execution.

## Evidence

Record inputs, compiled plan digest, selected tests and reasons, exact source identity,
timestamps and result state.

## Stop policy

Stop on dependency drift, nondeterminism, hidden action expansion, unsafe argv, source
mismatch, or optimistic handling of unknown mappings.
